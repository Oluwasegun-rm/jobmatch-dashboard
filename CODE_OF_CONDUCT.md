Code of Conduct
===============

We are committed to a harassment-free, inclusive environment. Be respectful, collaborative, and constructive.

We do not tolerate harassment or exclusionary behavior. This includes (but is not limited to):
- Offensive comments related to identity (gender, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, religion, or similar)
- Threats of violence, deliberate intimidation, stalking, sustained disruption
- Unwelcome sexual attention

Enforcement
- Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including temporary/permanent bans for violations.
- For private reports, contact the maintainers using the repository’s contact info.
